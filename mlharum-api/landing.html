THE PIPELINE

From Tree to Table in 3 Steps

📸
STEP 1
Farmer Scans

Farmer photographs mango with open palm as scale. MediaPipe detects 21 hand landmarks — no ruler needed.

🤖
STEP 2
AI Predicts

YOLOv8 detects the fruit. Server calculates size in cm, maps to growth stage, returns exact harvest date.

🛍️
STEP 3
Buyer Orders

Buyers browse live harvest data, pre-order, and receive a ripeness alert when their mango is ready.
